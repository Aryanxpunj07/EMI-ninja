About Us

At Evalora, we believe that smart financial decisions shouldn't be overly complicated or difficult to map out. That's why we created the Evalora EMI Calculator – to give everyday people the power to make responsible borrowing decisions with absolute clarity.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide intuitive, robust, and highly accurate financial planning tools that are completely free to use. From traditional flat-rate loans to complex step-up payment models, our robust calculator ensures you are never caught off guard by hidden fees or unexpected loan realities.

Who Developed This?

This tool is developed by a single financial enthusiasts and software developers who saw a dire need for a modern, ad-friendly, easy-to-use utility online.
