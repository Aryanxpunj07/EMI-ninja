Understanding EMI: Your Complete Guide

Welcome to the Evalora EMI Calculator. Whether you're planning to take out a home loan, looking for a new car, or securing a personal line of credit, understanding your Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) is the most critical step in financial planning. This tool provides an accurate and instant breakdown of your payment plan, allowing you to optimize your budget.

What is an EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment. It is a fixed payment amount made by a borrower to a lender at a specified date each calendar month. Equated monthly installments are used to pay off both interest and principal each month so that over a specified number of years, the loan is fully paid off along with interest.

How is EMI Calculated?

The mathematical formula used for calculating EMIs on a reducing balance is:

EMI = [P x R x (1+R)^N]/[(1+R)^N-1]

  • P stands for the Principal Loan Amount.
  • R is the monthly interest rate (Annual Rate / 12 / 100).
  • N is the loan tenure in months.

Benefits of Using Our Calculator

Our EMI calculator allows you to experiment with different Interest Types like Flat, Reducing, Step-Up, and Balloon payments. Understanding the difference between flat and reducing interest, for example, can save you thousands of rupees over the lifetime of a home loan.
